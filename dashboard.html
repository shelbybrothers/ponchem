Payment

Every docking test costs 100 $PONCHEM or 0.0001 ETH, paid to the lab treasury. The payer chooses in the lab.

While $PONCHEM is not launched only the ETH option works, and the token option reads after the $PONCHEM launch.

Prize pools are funded by sponsors only. A settled epoch pays the best wallet on that target, minus the lab fee.
